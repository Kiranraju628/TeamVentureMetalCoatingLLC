Our Foundation

Precision Engineering & Industrial Reliability

Delivering critical metal fabrication, corrosion protection, and piping solutions that endure the harsh operating environments of the Arabian Gulf.

Team Venture Metal Coating LLC – S.P.C. was established to meet the stringent technical demands of the UAE’s energy, marine, and construction sectors. Operating with state-of-the-art facilities in Musaffah Industrial Area (Abu Dhabi) and Sajja (Sharjah), our team combines advanced metallurgical knowledge, certified welding procedures, and specialized surface treatment technologies to execute complex contracts safely and on schedule.

ASME & AWS Compliance

Fully qualified WPS and PQR procedures for high-pressure spools.

Guaranteed Delivery Dates

Strict schedule management protecting major capital expenditures.

Certified Inspection & QA/QC

100% NDT (RT, UT, MPI, DPT) and hydrostatic testing with Mill Test Certificates.

Global Alloy Sourcing

Direct supply partnerships for seamless pipes, fittings, and flanges.

Quality Management

Stringent Engineering Standards

Every spool, skid, and coated component manufactured in our yards undergoes rigorous multi-tier inspection before dispatch.

ASME B31.3 Process Piping

All pressure pipe spools are fabricated in strict compliance with ASME B31.3 and ASME Section IX welding qualifications, backed by complete welding traceability.

AWS D1.1 Structural Welding

Structural steel frames, equipment skids, and modular assemblies welded according to AWS D1.1 structural welding standards with verified magnetic particle and ultrasonic inspection.

NACE / SSPC Surface Preparation

Surface blast cleaning to Sa 2.5 standard followed by multi-layer epoxy, polyurethane, or thermal spray aluminum (TSA) application verified by certified coating inspectors.
